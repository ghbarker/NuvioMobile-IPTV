Nuvio IPTV

An Android media app based on the open-source Nuvio Mobile, with Live TV features for your own IPTV playlists.

What it does

  • Plays your own M3U or Xtream playlists with a TV guide, catch-up, On Demand and recordings
  • Optionally backs up your Live TV setup to your own Google Drive
  • Optionally syncs watch history with Trakt or Simkl

The app does not provide any channels or content. You add your own sources.
