Privacy policy

Nuvio IPTV · Last updated November 2, 2026

Summary

Nuvio IPTV has no servers of its own and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your data. Everything the app stores stays on your device, or in accounts you choose to connect.

Data stored on your device

Your playlists and their logins, TV guide sources, favorites, settings, recordings and watch progress are stored only on your device.

Google Drive (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks for the drive.file permission. This lets it create and read only the files the app itself created in your Drive. It cannot see any of your other files. The app uses this to save one file, "Nuvio Live TV backup.json", containing your Live TV setup (including your playlist logins), and to read it back when you restore. The file lives in your own Google Drive; the developer never receives it. You can sign out in the app and delete the file from your Drive at any time.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Trakt and Simkl (optional)

If you connect Trakt or Simkl, the app stores a sign-in token on your device and sends your watch activity to that service so it can track your history. Their own privacy policies apply to what they store.

Nuvio account (optional)

If you sign in with a Nuvio account, the app uses the Nuvio service for account features such as profiles and sync, under Nuvio's own terms.

Content

The app connects directly to the playlist, guide and stream addresses you add. Those providers may see your IP address, as with any streaming app.
